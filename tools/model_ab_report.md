  not estimates. Machine otherwise idle; no test suites ran concurrently.
- **`qwen3-4b-nothink` was requested but is NOT POSSIBLE**: the installed
  `qwen3:4b` is the 2507-thinking build (262k context). Its thinking cannot be
  disabled — verified live: a template no-think prefill AND Ollama's native
  `think:false` both leave it narrating its deliberation into `content`. The
  benchmark therefore ran **stock `qwen3:4b` (thinking)** as the labeled
  substitute; a true no-think 4B would be the separate `qwen3:4b-instruct-2507`
  tag (not pulled — owner's call).

  Thinking is routed to the `reasoning` field, but the surviving `content`
  failed `validate_response` every time. All its explanations came from
  nothing — honest "(none generated)" — so its latency numbers are of
  failures, not answers. Not a candidate as installed.
- **`qwen3-8b-nothink` was the most reliable: 6/6 shape-valid replies**
  (llama3.1:8b: 3/4 — one wrong-shaped reply on OpenSSH_2k). Its clean
  Linux_2k run also had the best wall time (74.6s) because nothing needed
  the re-ask pass. Warm per-call medians overlap llama's (llama ~54s,
  qwen3-8b-nothink ~67-74s) and include queueing: explanation calls run 2-wide
  here (2 eager chunks, LLM_WORKERS=3 cap).
- The analyzer's `preflight` prints a cosmetic warning that `qwen3-8b-nothink`
  is "not listed" — it compares exact names and Ollama lists the tag as
  `qwen3-8b-nothink:latest`. The run proceeds and works; only the model_ab
  availability check normalizes the suffix.
- Explanations are mostly "(none generated)" by design: the app explains
  eagerly only the top-3 findings (+ a bounded re-ask pass); the rest are
  on-demand surfaces. That is the production behavior being measured.
